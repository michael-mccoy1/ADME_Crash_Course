# Writing style

## Audience

Working DMPK scientists. Do not define clearance, volume of distribution, fraction unbound, extraction ratio or other standard terms. State the method, its assumptions and its measured accuracy.

## Tone

* Neutral, precise, concise technical prose. Impersonal. Active voice. Short sentences.
* No analogies, rhetorical questions, exclamations or second-person storytelling.
* Forbidden: why this matters, imagine, think of, notice how, let's, simply, just, powerful, crucial, key insight, robust, "it turns out", "the idea is", "under the hood".
* Do not state that something is easy or intuitive. Do not praise.
* Give numbers with their source. Say which data set a result comes from.
* Spelling: American (ionization, summarize, modeled).
* Typography: no em dashes, en dashes, middle dots, multiplication signs or ellipsis characters.

## Numbers

* Every number in a lesson comes from a published table or from a calculation in this repository. Record the source.
* Quote reported values as reported. If this repository recalculates a value and the result differs from the paper, state both.

## Titles

Sentence case, noun phrases, six words or fewer, no colons, no questions.

## Interface text

* Try lines: one or two imperative sentences naming the control.
* Notice lines: one factual statement about the figure.
* Quiz questions: one precise question with a short precise answer.
